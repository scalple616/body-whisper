# Body Whisper · 身体低语

**A sensory self-awareness trainer — read the signals your body sends before your mind notices them.**

一个感官自察训练器：在念头察觉之前，读懂身体发来的信号。

> Piloerection (goosebumps), a held breath, sudden warmth — these are autonomic fingerprints of emotional arousal. They are *not* under conscious control. That's exactly why they're worth recording.

---

## Why this exists

Almost every AI product today tries to model the human **mind**. Almost none of them listen to the human **body**.

Body Whisper is a small experiment in that direction: a zero-cost, zero-server, privacy-first web app that helps you

1. **Train vagal tone** with paced resonance breathing (~6 breaths/min — textbook-supported),
2. **Log body signals** the moment they happen (goosebumps, breath shifts, heart awareness…),
3. **Discover patterns** — which environments light *your* body up, revealed by your own data after 20+ entries.

## Features

- **Breath Pacer** — 5s in / 5s out visual breathing circle, optional gentle audio cues (432 Hz / 336 Hz). Based on the repeatedly validated resonance-frequency breathing protocol.
- **Sensory Diary** — one-tap logging: body signals, intensity (1–5), environment tags, optional note. Takes 30 seconds.
- **Pattern Mirror** — bar-chart analysis of which environments most often trigger your signals. Your body's own "nourishment list".
- **Honest Science Layer** — the app visibly separates *textbook consensus* from *theoretical hypotheses* (e.g. polyvagal theory is labeled as contested). Real is the criterion of beauty.

## Privacy

All data stays in your browser's `localStorage`. No account, no server, no upload, no third party. Clearing browser data clears your diary — by design.

## Quick start

1. Open `index.html` in any modern browser — that's it.
2. Or host it anywhere static (GitHub Pages, etc.).

## Science footnotes

**Textbook consensus** (safe to rely on):
- Piloerection is a **sympathetic** pathway (α1 receptors on arrector pili muscles) — not vagal, and not under conscious control.
- The **vagus nerve** is the main parasympathetic trunk; its tone is clinically measured via heart-rate variability (HRV).
- Paced breathing at ~6 breaths/min increases vagal tone — validated in multiple studies and adopted in cardiac rehabilitation.

**Theoretical hypotheses** (proceed with a lamp):
- Polyvagal theory (Porges) is inspiring but contested in academia.
- "Sustained vagal stimulation → stronger cognition & longevity" is a directional inference, not settled science.

This app is a mirror and a diary — **not medical advice**.
